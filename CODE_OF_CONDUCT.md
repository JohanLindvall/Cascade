# Code of Conduct

Cascade welcomes people of every background and experience level. This applies to issues,
pull requests, code reviews and other project spaces, including people representing the project.

## Working together

- Be considerate and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Critique ideas and code respectfully. Explain a disagreement and leave room for a different view.
- Keep feedback relevant and actionable. Respect other people's time and boundaries.
- Give credit for other people's work and respect their privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks and publishing someone's
private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruption or continuing
unwanted contact after being asked to stop is not acceptable either.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainer, Johan Lindvall, privately at
[johan.lindvall@gmail.com](mailto:johan.lindvall@gmail.com). Include links or context where
possible and say how you would prefer to be contacted. You do not need to confront someone or
make a public report.

Reports will be handled as privately as possible, sharing information only where needed to
address the concern. Responses are best effort. If a report concerns the maintainer or needs
GitHub's intervention, you can also use
[GitHub's abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, ask for a change in behaviour, or restrict
participation temporarily or permanently, depending on the severity and repetition of the
conduct. Anyone affected may use the same private contact to provide context or request review.

For a software vulnerability, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
